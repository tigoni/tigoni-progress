# Kijani MRV | Trustless MRV & Carbon Marketplace

## Project Manifesto

### Introduction
Kijani MRV addresses the critical lack of transparency and trust in the voluntary carbon market. By leveraging the Cardano blockchain, we provide a decentralized platform for the Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) of climate impact data.

Our mission is to ensure that every carbon credit represents a real, verifiable, and permanent reduction in atmospheric CO2. Through smart contracts and on-chain verification, we eliminate the risk of double-counting and greenwashing, empowering both project developers and climate-conscious investors.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Trustless MRV Workflow** - Automated and tamper-proof verification of climate data using on-chain validators.
- **Cardano Native Tokens** - Verified carbon credits issued as native assets on the Cardano blockchain.
- **Decentralized Marketplace** - Peer-to-peer trading of carbon credits without intermediaries.
- **Proof of Verification** - Transparent audit trails for every credit, from project inception to retirement.
- **Climate Impact Dashboard** - Real-time monitoring of project performance and carbon sequestration metrics.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **On-Chain Logic:** Aiken (Plutus V2 smart contracts)
- **Backend Infrastructure:** Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL
- **Blockchain Interface:** Blockfrost, Lucid
- **DevOps:** Docker, GitHub Actions
- **Infrastructure:** AWS
